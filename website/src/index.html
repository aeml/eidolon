04 / Before you set out

How to play
Eidolon.

Eidolon is in active development. The world, its systems, and its balance continue to evolve.

What is Eidolon?+

Eidolon is a browser-based, real-time multiplayer action RPG with four player classes, elemental realms, quests, dungeons, and persistent character progression.

How do I start playing?+

Head to play.eidolonrealms.com, follow the account and character setup, and choose Fighter, Rogue, Wizard, or Cleric. Once in town, open your quests to find your next objective.

Do I need to download anything?+

No separate game client is needed. Eidolon runs in your web browser and requires an internet connection to play.

Can I play with friends?+

Yes. Eidolon includes multiplayer gameplay, friends, and parties so you can connect with other players and head out together.
