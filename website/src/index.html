01 / The world

Four elemental realms.
One broken covenant.

Eidolon takes its name from the four great spirits who shaped its world: Orun of Root and Stone, Neris of Tide and Memory, Pyralis of Flame and Will, and Aeral of Sky and Freedom. Their crystals keep the elements in a covenant that shelters mortal life.

Malachar, the Dark King, has wounded that covenant. Shelter becomes obedience, memory becomes a chain, and gifts freely given become instruments of control. Your journey begins in Lanternhold, where Archmage Ilyra asks you to uncover what went wrong.

The Fourfold Chronicle is a 55-chapter campaign of realm investigations, recovered relics, discoveries, and conversations. Follow the trail through Earth, Water, Fire, and Air. Defeat each dungeon’s corrupted guardian, open the way to its separate crystal raid, and defend Artificer Maelin while the crystal is repaired.

04 / In the alpha

An adventure with
room to make it yours.

These systems are implemented in the alpha. Content, balance, reliability, and polish are still being tested and improved; individual features may have rough edges.

01

Real-time combat & builds

Play Fighter, Rogue, Wizard, or Cleric. Develop abilities and talents, shape your specialization, and combine damage, control, defense, and support with your party.

02

A world with a story

Follow the Fourfold Chronicle through four elemental zones and the Dark Realm. Investigate disturbances, recover relics, read discoveries in your Journal, and take optional daily contracts.

03

Dungeons & crystal raids

Delve into five instanced dungeons and four elemental crystal raids. Fight through their guardians, then hold the line during Maelin’s three-wave repair Vigils.

04

Level 100 & beyond

Reach the Dark Realm expedition and the weekly 5–10-player Dark King raid. Continue developing your character after level 100 through Resonance ranks and traits.

05

Loot, equipment & the Forge

Collect equipment, refine your build at the Forge, and store your finds in the stash. Equipment appearances bring your gear into the world, while upgrades give your next expedition a purpose.

06

Friends, parties & guilds

Meet players through chat and Group Finder. Coordinate party roles and ready checks, build a friends list, and organize a guild with shared storage, calendars, and guild chat.

07

A player economy

Trade directly with other players or browse the Trading House. Buy, sell, and prepare in town as the alpha’s loot rewards, upgrade costs, and economy continue to be tuned.

08

Competitive play, by choice

Challenge another player to a duel, enter 1v1 or 2v2 arenas, or opt into overworld PvP. Ranked seasons, leaderboards, and cosmetic-focused rewards give rivals something to pursue.

09

Life in Lanternhold

Recover health and mana, build up Well Rested time, and prepare with merchants. The optional two-floor Casino includes slots, blackjack, Hold’em, roulette, and baccarat. Public tables use Gold; VIP tables use EP. EP buys cosmetics, never player power, and cannot convert back to Gold. Real-money purchases are not available.

10

A persistent browser world

Return to saved characters and reconnect to your adventure. Desktop and touch controls, configurable quest tracking, world maps, and in-game reporting support everyday play. Phone performance and interface polish remain active work.

06 / Before you set out

How to play
Eidolon.

Start in Lanternhold. Find your calling. Follow Ilyra’s first signal into the realms.

What is Eidolon?+

Eidolon is an independent, real-time multiplayer browser action RPG in open alpha. It combines four character classes, a story across four elemental realms and the Dark Realm, dungeons, raids, loot, guilds, trading, PvP, and persistent progression.

Is Eidolon finished?+

No. The game is playable in open alpha and is constantly being improved. Expect bugs, unfinished visuals, balance changes, and ongoing content and interface work. Closed beta and full release are planned milestones, with no announced launch date. See the development roadmap for the direction.

How do I start playing?+

Head to play.eidolonrealms.com, follow the account and character setup, and choose Fighter, Rogue, Wizard, or Cleric. In Lanternhold, speak with Archmage Ilyra and accept the Fourfold Chronicle. Use your Journal and quest tracker to follow the story; daily contracts are optional.

Do I need to download anything?+

No separate game client is needed. Eidolon runs in your web browser and requires an internet connection to play.

Can I play with friends or explore on my own?+

You can explore and progress your character on your own, and connect with others through friends, parties, guilds, chat, and Group Finder. Group content becomes a central part of the adventure, including the elemental raids and the 5–10-player Dark King raid.

Can I play on a phone?+

Eidolon includes responsive interfaces and touch controls alongside desktop controls. Mobile playability and performance are still being refined during alpha; the experience can vary by device and browser, especially in busy group encounters.

How do I reach the Dark Realm?+

Progress through the Fourfold Chronicle, reach level 100, and complete all four elemental crystal repairs. The restored crystals open the Dark Realm expedition. Follow its story and clear the Umbral Nexus before facing Malachar in the Dark King raid.

Where can I report a bug or see updates?+

Use the in-game report tool for bugs and feedback. The login screen contains versioned patch notes so you can check what changed in the current build. The roadmap above describes broader development priorities.
