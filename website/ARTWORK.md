# Website artwork

`artwork/realms-hero.png` was created with the built-in ImageGen tool for this website. It is atmospheric concept art, not a gameplay screenshot. The live page labels it accordingly. The website serves compressed WebP copies at 1536 and 960 pixels wide; the PNG original is not included in the public build.

Final generation prompt:

> Sophisticated cinematic painterly dark fantasy landscape for Eidolon: ancient ruined stone portal on the right with antique gold light, winding stone path, misty mountain valley, pine forests, distant embers and icy peaks. Wide composition with subdued dark left 45% for website text. Charcoal, muted forest green, slate and antique gold; restrained, atmospheric, elegant. No text, logos, watermark, UI, people, characters, or screenshot.

`src/assets/gameplay.webp` and `src/assets/gameplay-640.webp` are compressed versions of the repository's existing town screenshot, `docs/media/gameplay-overworld.png`, at 1280 and 640 pixels wide. Only encoding and resolution changed; the content is unchanged.
