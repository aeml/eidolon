## Changes

- The Journal adds an optional, collapsed “Your journey so far” recap. It
  distinguishes opening dungeon roads from actually repairing crystals, explains
  the shared expedition versus private court, and points to the earned epilogue.
- The most recent personally completed expedition account gives returning
  players context without revealing uncompleted chapters. Authored order, not a
  saved chapter number or objective count, determines that account.
- Recap statements require personal completed receipts; ready quests, another
  party member's access or optional catch-up chapters cannot imply other progress.
  Open controls, text and reading position survive ordinary Journal refresh.
- Ilyra's post-Nexus greeting and server Nexus/finale descriptions name
  Lanternhold's Dungeon Guide and personal Complete Quest. They no longer imply
  the Fourfold Portal enters the throne room. Existing contracts retain counts,
  quoted rewards (including zero) and previously granted rewards.
