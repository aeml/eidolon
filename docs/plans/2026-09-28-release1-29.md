## Player-facing changes

- Eidolon aid no longer disappears when targets change or replaces a danger
  warning. A bounded independent notice shows the received phase; full dialogue
  and effects remain in the Game log. Notices clear on death, disconnect,
  leaving the instance and teardown. They do not guess a phase after reconnect.
- Phones briefly use the quest-tracker space for a compact phase/aid summary,
  then restore the tracker. Desktop shows the full effect beside combat feedback.
  Quest data, selected tracking and combat controls are unchanged.
- Brief stone, water, fire and air cues accompany received aid around your
  living character. They preserve world height, use fewer motes on Low, create
  no misleading ground danger circles and expire through normal effect cleanup.
- The Guide explains all four interventions, role opportunities, movement
  warnings and the personal finale claim. Aid does not mean invulnerability or
  resurrection, and phase four is not victory.
- A cleared court with your finale actually ready directs you to Ilyra for
  Complete Quest and the rereadable epilogue. Repeat runs retain ordinary exit
  guidance; weekly cache eligibility is separate and unchanged.
- The Journal requires your completed finale receipt before declaring victory.
  A partial list of finished early chapters now points back to Ilyra rather than
  incorrectly claiming Malachar has fallen. Ready-but-unclaimed is not complete.
