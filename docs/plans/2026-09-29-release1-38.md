# Alpha 1.38 — party presence and rewards

Isolated candidate versioned 1.38.0; not published or accepted live.

- Disconnected characters retained for reconnect no longer receive new
  dungeon room-clear rewards or shrine healing while offline.
- Zero-health characters awaiting their DEAD-state transition cannot receive
  shrine healing or room bonuses either; shrines do not resurrect them.
- Reconnecting does not replay already-cleared room rewards. Existing party
  membership and dungeon-resume windows remain unchanged.
- Connected party members still share kill credit throughout their dungeon;
  downed allies count. Overworld sharing retains its roughly two-screen range.
  Quest rewards still require each player's own manual turn-in.
- Party guidance distinguishes downed-eligible kill credit from living-only
  room bonuses and shrine effects.
- Downed allies still gain earned XP and levels, but leveling up no longer
  fills their health bar while they remain dead. Living level-up healing is
  unchanged; normal respawn rules still apply.

The [party review](2026-09-29-1-38-party-progression-preflight.md) records the
reproduced failure, scoped regression evidence and remaining review work.
