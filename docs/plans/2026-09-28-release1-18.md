  aligned woven edging. No cloth simulation or animation timing change.

  relief maps, shared by all items rather than allocated per character. Twelve

  not metallic. Rarity accents, sockets, item and cosmetic identities unchanged.

  crown/band; level1/30/100 eye-clearance checks and actual browser close-up pass.

  outside walking routes and environmental warning clearances. Aprons stay below
  path overlays, use no depth writes and create no second walk floor. Chips stay
  below.3m. No new solid, spawn exclusion, investigation, reward or saved bit.
  Flood Shelter's near-hazard apron is intentionally smaller rather than covering
  the warning. Existing Earth ground dressing is retained.
- Minimap uses the atlas's Dark Realm court markers, filters and instance/loaded
