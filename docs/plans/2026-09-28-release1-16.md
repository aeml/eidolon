## Delivered implementation

- Eight places per realm: two dressed existing story compositions, two landmarks
  including the preserved dungeon approach, two abandoned camps/workspaces and
  two functional optional lore sites. Water uses gauges, moorings, ribs and sail
  cloth; Fire uses communal kilns, cooling channels, furnaces and broken seals.
  These are the population first pass, not finished blockbuster-quality art.
- Four original three-paragraph readings extend the story's care-versus-control
  themes. Streamed passive objects use the existing range/instance/life checks,
  E/normal interaction/mobile USE and owned dialogs. No new quests, grants,
  auto-completion, saved discovery flags or spoilers at locked story markers.
- Sixteen physical path ribbons share exact centerlines with atlas cartography.
  Branches avoid canonical hazards and entrance/building bounds. Public map and
  radar destinations use real readings or exterior gathering points, not solid
  building centers. Camps and paths remain combat territory.
- Deterministic Water/Fire foliage clearings expose sites and paths without
  adding tree collision.62 new scenery solids bring the generated world spawn
  registry to89 and public reading count to6. Client movement footprints, server
  spawn exclusions and administrator landing exclusions are regenerated together.
  Water/Fire have separate spawners: those now use bounded clear-position rolls;
  all1200 ordinary Water/1000 Fire enemies retain their counts. Existing authored
  combat anchors are not counted as ordinary regional rolls or relocated.
- Muted frost/volcanic ground replaces bright repeating crack networks. Albedo,
  relief and roughness are deterministic, quality-registered and resource-owned.
  No new lights, external textures, per-frame texture generation or actors.
- Failed/rejected reconnect now restores the actual hidden login ancestor,
  retires retries/socket/old authentication state, clears the password and
  preserves username. Socket/Blob/load callbacks are fenced to their owning
  game. Resume tokens stay in page memory instead of borrowing another tab's
  shared token; server token/session authority is unchanged.
- Optional browser preference reads/writes tolerate denied storage. HUD and
  touch controls remain usable, in-session settings still apply, and an unsaved
  graphics setting cannot prompt a pointless reload. Login help explains retries,
  session replacement, Menu return,15-minute dungeon expiry and current support
  limitations. No invented password-reset service or account privilege.
