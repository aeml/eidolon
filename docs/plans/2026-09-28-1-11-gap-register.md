## Evidence and status rules

- **Retain** means existing code/checks and documented release evidence cover
  the stated foundation. Reuse them unless changed; it does not prove bug-free
  play, human pacing or future visual acceptance.
- **Local** means implemented/checked in the work branch, not production.
- **Gap** means a confirmed missing/stale feature or a newly required workstream.
- **Owner** means an explicit external input/decision; never silently waive it.
- Implementation owner is the roadmap agent unless a row names the user/operator.
  Severity: **B** beta requirement, **L** later launch requirement, **P** polish,
  **O** owner-only evidence/decision. A newly discovered security/save/reward
  blocker always overrides this schedule.
