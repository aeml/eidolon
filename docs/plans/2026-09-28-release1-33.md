# Alpha 1.33 — builds that work together

Release candidate versioned 1.33.0, not yet deployed or accepted live. Prior
1.32 delivery is accepted. This package excludes later inventory/Forge/economy
work; exact-candidate verification, CI and public release checks remain.

- Talent Master reset prices and affordability now match what the server
  actually charges, including each twenty-level price boundary.
- Existing reset prices, point refunds, free talent reset and saved builds are
  unchanged. This corrects misleading display and disabled buttons, not costs.
- Five combo descriptions now explain actual benefits and limits, including
  nearby living-only healing and the distinction between longer protection and
  forced enemy attention.
- Initial Wizard B rework: Scorch Beam → Arcane Missiles gives a five-missile
  Arcane Barrage at ordinary cost/cooldown. The old cross-branch shield-to-meteor
  pair is retired. Spell Focus adds a brief non-stacking ward alongside its
  next-spell charge, without replacing active shields. Skill IDs and learned
  selections remain unchanged. The rest of the full-kit work is still pending.
- Scorch Beam slows susceptible targets briefly without overwriting existing
  slows, piercing walls or affecting allies in offline play.
- Time Warp refreshes your own Teleport and Gravity Well, not other party
  members' skills or itself. The hotbar receives the authoritative refresh
  immediately. Normal mana and global-cooldown rules still apply.
- Wizard combos now fit learned branches: Fireball → Flame Whip and Flame
  Tornado → Inferno Cataclysm for Pyromancer; Scorch Beam → Arcane Missiles for
  precision; Gravity Well → Fireball for control. Implosion also benefits hits
  against control-immune enemies without removing their immunity.
- Initial offensive Fighter change: Last Stand Rampage can be activated at
  ordinary health for 2× Damage stat, retaining 3× below 30% health. Mastery
  strengthens either value. Strength is captured when cast; duration and
  cooldown rules are unchanged. Fighter's full-kit acceptance remains open.
- Iron Will now belongs to the offensive Fighter branch: Berserker Edge →
  Last Stand Rampage grants a non-stacking 20%-maximum-health ward for Rampage's
  duration. It no longer borrows Iron Fortress's armor or movement penalty.
- Unbreakable Grip now strikes its target and generates double-damage threat,
  making it useful against bosses without bypassing control immunity. Pull,
  root, targeting and mastery-duration rules are preserved for living targets.
- Offline Guardian Combo and Tremor Rush now deliver the same extended
  protection and control-immune-safe knockdown as their server counterparts.
- Shield Fighter gains Fortress Counter: Iron Fortress refreshes Shield Slam;
  following with Shield Slam inside the combo window adds 50% damage. Normal
  mana/global cooldown, targeting, immunity and rune rules remain. The hotbar
  receives the authoritative refresh immediately; rejected guards do not reset it.
- Juggernaut Charge now actually charges up to 10 units before releasing its
  damaging, slowing shockwave. Movement stops at dungeon walls; the impact
  circle appears at the landing, not prematurely at the casting position.
  Mana, cooldown, trained shockwave damage/radius and control immunity remain.
- Rogue combos now stay within their branches: Ambush uses Weak Point Mark →
  Backstab; Venom Burst uses Poison Coating → Tripwire for double trap impact
  damage; Shadow Dance uses Cloak & Vanish → Smoke Bomb to refresh Tripwire.
  Shadow Dance now pays normal Smoke mana/cooldown instead of the old discount.
- Tripwire can be aimed within six units on reachable ground instead of being
  placed only at your feet. Its root still respects immunity. Smoke's trap
  refresh immediately updates the authoritative hotbar.
- Throwing Rogue gains a spacing window: while Serrated Edges is active,
  Fan of Knives slows susceptible enemies by 20% for two seconds base without
  replacing an existing slow. The Weighted rune retains its 30%/three-second
  effect. Offline Blade Tornado now actually pierces multiple enemies, and
  delayed Volley shots stop after cancellation, death or changing zones.
- Cleric combos now fit their branches: Healing Light → Guardian Embrace
  grants the caster three seconds of damage immunity base (not the party);
  Consecrated Ground → Radiant Strike doubles the strike without requiring
  a cross-branch enemy mark; Blessing of Zeal → Spirit Guardians activates
  boosted guardians. Divine Intervention → Healing Light remains living-only
  area healing. Normal costs and cooldowns apply.
- Blessing of Zeal refreshes only the caster's Spirit Guardians cooldown,
  making the support payoff immediately available after the global cooldown.
  The authoritative hotbar updates immediately; allies' skills are unchanged.
- Purifying Wave retains its ally cleanse and gains a modest holy pulse
  (20 + Wisdom before modifiers) against nearby enemies, with a 30% slow for
  two seconds base. Hostile effects respect dungeon walls, immunity and
  existing slows. Mastery expands both pulse and cleanse radius.
- Offline Cleric now implements the protective and boosted-guardian combo
  effects and their feedback. Mark of Weakness rejects allies, invalid range,
  blocked targets and foreign/authoritative actors before spending mana.
- Combo cards on desktop and phones now identify the required specialization,
  unlock level and whether the pair is learned in your build. Juggernaut's
  targeting range now matches its revised ten-unit charge limit.
