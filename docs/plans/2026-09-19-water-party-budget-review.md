This identifies party pacing as a remaining measurement priority, not proof
that the campaign is blocked. The omitted dungeon and incidental encounters
matter, and a party can kill faster than a solo character. Earlier Earth-only
budget diagnostics already show the same shared-XP effect; retain the accepted
solo Earth readiness playthrough rather than repeat it.

The full earned party progression check must record actual entry level, dungeon
income, extra kills/time and rest usage before concluding whether additional
story objectives or reward tuning is needed. Prepared level70 raid characters
cannot establish this. Do not inflate rewards, remove party XP sharing, require
dailies or add the rejected level-based stat multiplier from this model alone.
