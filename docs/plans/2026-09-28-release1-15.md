## Shared content and physical-path checkpoint

- `worldPopulation.js` defines eight distinct Earth compositions with roles,
  footprint radii, visual recipes and public/quest visibility policies. Existing
  house/scar/entrance anchors are resolved from actual investigation/entrance
  sources, not a second coordinate list. Existing quest IDs/masks/rewards stay
  unchanged. New location scenery and interactions are **not yet implemented**.
- Two authored optional readings concern the bellkeeper's unconditional shelter
  and the travelers' defense of their oath. These are content, not saved discovery
  flags, quest completions or rewards. They still need physical interaction/UI.
- Eleven physical path ribbons are attached during current overworld decoration
  loading. Cached Earth cartography draws those same centerlines. This is the
  first physical Earth-road network, not a claim that earlier clear strips were
  already rendered roads. Branches bypass existing trunks; the Bastion branch
  stops before its preserved footprint and a northern bypass reaches the realm
  boundary. The diary spur bends around the house to its south-facing doorway;
  the initially proposed direct spur would have crossed the north wall.
- Shared 128px generated loam/rut texture, standard lighting/shadows/fog,
  world-distance UVs, continuous mitered joins, no additional colliders, no new
  assets or per-frame texture work. Eleven draws and fewer than500 vertices for
  the network before renderer culling; one scene-owned material/texture.
  These are structural costs, **not a measured full-scene frame budget**.
- Focused tests cover roles/content, canonical anchors, all route-to-tree and
  hazard margins, sampled house/entrance footprint avoidance, upward mesh normals,
  joins, bounded geometry, invalid inputs, exact atlas centerlines and stale-load
  attachment guards. Production scenery/visual review is still required; passing
  geometry tests is not acceptance of the finished populated world.
- Node24 focused run:32 checks across `WorldPopulation`, `AtlasCartography` and
  `WorldGeneratorCanonicalGeometry` passed in4.537s; touched-file ESLint and
  whitespace checks pass. An initial run used the shell's older Node and hit an
  existing `toSorted` compatibility error; reran under the required Node24 rather
  than changing supported-runtime expectations. The initial new clearance check
  caught the diary spur ending too close to its wall; moved the endpoint one
  metre outward. Atlas float comparison uses an8-decimal tolerance.
