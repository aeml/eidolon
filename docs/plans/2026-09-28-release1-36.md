# Alpha 1.36 — Gold economy safety

Isolated candidate versioned 1.36.0; not published or accepted live.

- Vendor buyback restores equipment into an available visible bag slot.
  A full bag no longer charges Gold for equipment placed beyond its capacity.
- Rejected buyback preserves the recovery item and balance; successful
  recovery preserves the item's full equipment properties.
- Legacy no-database auction overflow keeps items on the player's ground plane
  and in their instance. Durable auction bag/stash settlement is unchanged.
- Normal server shutdown flushes the last partial hour of economy metrics,
  preserving observations across ordinary deployments shorter than one hour.
  These aggregate logs are not a crash-proof transaction ledger.

No prices, drop rates or existing balances changed. The malformed vendor-sale
guard was delivered earlier with 1.31, not held for this milestone.
See the [economy review](2026-09-28-1-36-gold-economy-preflight.md) for scoped
test evidence and isolated packaging checks. Ordered publication, CI and live
verification remain. No live inflation or pacing verdict yet.
