# Alpha 1.32 — warnings that belong to your encounter

Local implementation candidate; no version bump, publication or acceptance.
1.30 is accepted live.1.31 is published at36c82ebd, CI36493779845 queued;
wait for terminal success and independent live verification before delivery.
Later1.33–1.36 changes are excluded from this package.

- Boss warnings retain their authored movement advice instead of falling back
  to generic impact text. Multi-circle attacks show all boundaries but announce
  their warning once.
- Warning events are scoped to their original dungeon or overworld instance.
  Late warnings from a different area are ignored after travel.
- In the shared overworld, distant warnings outside the loaded area no longer
  replace local combat feedback. Large circles overlapping your area remain.
- Offline Seraphs cannot attack while stunned. Stun expiry restores attacks
  without extending lifetime or preventing owner-loss cleanup.

No damage, radius, windup, stat scaling or reward changes. See the
[fairness preflight](2026-09-28-1-32-combat-fairness-preflight.md) for reproduced
failures, passing bounded checks and evidence limits. Final integration,
version/patch-note packaging, CI and exact live verification remain.
