# Alpha 1.37 — clearer level-cap rewards

Isolated candidate versioned 1.37.0; not pushed or deployed.

- Boss and dungeon-room summaries distinguish ordinary XP from Resonance XP.
- A reward that takes you to level 100 shows both parts correctly, even if
  the character update arrives before or after the reward message.
- Existing reward amounts, progression rates and saved progress are unchanged.
- Recorded weekly-cache entitlements recover across disconnects and interrupted
  saves without duplicate grants. Legacy completed lockouts remain completed.
- An undeliverable recipient no longer prevents other pending caches or
  unrelated logins; its entitlement remains available for retry.
- The character sheet explains earned Resonance, optional daily quests and
  why EP cannot buy character-power trait points.

- Eligible completions use the character-save journal before requesting the
  weekly entitlement, allowing initial database-write failures to recover
  using the original completion week. Offline delivery restores normal bag
  capacity before deciding whether full-bag compensation is needed.

The [review](2026-09-29-1-37-resonance-preflight.md) records scoped checks and
reward-source and cosmetic-boundary review. CI and live verification remain.

## Migration and rollback boundary

This release introduces schema 15 and weekly receipt/outbox indexes, with no
reward backfill or balance reset. Use the deployment's existing backup and
schema preflight. Once schema 15 is applied, schema-14 writers (including 1.36)
must remain fenced: do not force an older binary onto the newer database or
erase the schema marker. Prefer a schema-15-compatible fix-forward; a restore
is a separately authorized recovery operation, not an automatic downgrade.
Disposable migration/restart evidence is in the linked review. No production
migration, database restore or reward grant has been performed in preparation.
