## Candidate changes

- **Stay for the repair.** When the assault is cleared but the crystal still
  reports fractured, the tracker directs the group to Maelin's Vigil instead
  of the generic dungeon exit message. A preparing ritual no longer pretends
  wave one has already started. Existing active-wave and regroup guidance stays.
- **Know your raid's job.** Each unlocked elemental raid card has a collapsible
  guardian/repair/reward briefing. It explains the guardian's actual warning
  pattern, the realm-specific ritual and the requirement to finish both the
  ritual task and all attackers in all three waves. Live ground markings remain
  authoritative; these instructions do not grant access or modify combat.
- **Maelin repairs; Ilyra handles quests.** The Guide explains that Maelin
  channels automatically and is not another quest giver. After restoration,
  the tracker says “Return to Ilyra in town.” Detailed instructions explain
  personal Complete Quest claims and distinguish quest rewards from raid loot.
  The compact title stays readable on phones, where both hint lines are hidden.
- **Progress is preserved.** Entry/ready-check requirements, claimed rewards,
  saved progress, normal dungeon exits and server repair/re-entry rules remain
  unchanged. No balance, XP, currency, drop-rate or save migration.
