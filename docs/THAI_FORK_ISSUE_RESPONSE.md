# Suggested response to issue #20

Thank you for asking and for your interest in bringing Eidolon to Thai players.
I've added an explicit [license](https://github.com/aeml/eidolon/blob/master/LICENSE)
and [licensing guide](https://github.com/aeml/eidolon/blob/master/docs/LICENSING.md)
to clarify these permissions.

1. **Translation:** Yes, you may translate the UI, menus, items, quests, and all
   other player-facing text into Thai or any other language and share that version.
2. **Code and features:** Yes, you may modify the source and add features. When
   you distribute your version or open your server to outside players, you must
   publish the complete matching editable source under the same license and
   notify this repository through an issue or pull request with the source link.
   Keep that submission or its linked release history current with your updates.
   This makes your changes available for reuse by the original project; it does
   not mean every change will be merged.
3. **Hosting:** Yes, you may operate your own Thai-language server in Thailand.
4. **Attribution:** Please retain the license and existing notices, and include
   "Based on Eidolon by Robert Mendola, original developer and maintainer" in
   an accessible credits/about screen and on your public server website, linking
   to this repository where supported. You may translate the credit and credit
   your own contributors too. Clearly identify the server as independently operated.
5. **Player models:** Yes, you may replace them with entirely new 3D models.
   Share the editable source projects, rigs, textures, animations, and exports
   under the license so the original project can also use them.
6. **Other assets:** The same permission and sharing requirements apply to
   monsters, weapons, armor, equipment, mounts, and other replacement game assets.

**Commercial activity is permitted**, including virtual-item sales, cosmetic
outfits, mounts, convenience items, monthly VIP subscriptions, and third-party
payment integrations. No separate project permission or revenue share is needed
if you comply with the license. Your integrated payment code must be shared;
credentials, private keys, player information, and transaction records must stay
private. Independent payment providers' infrastructure need not be published.

Official Eidolon's philosophy is that money buys cosmetics, never in-game power.
I will not merge changes that sell gameplay advantages. Your independent fork
may choose a different monetization model, but it must still share those changes
under the license, even if they are not accepted upstream.

Please provide players with a prominent source link and retain source versions
for the period specified in the license. Use only replacement assets you have
the rights to share in editable form and license for upstream reuse; existing
third-party licenses and notices still apply. You remain responsible for your
payment-provider agreements and applicable laws.

The project now uses a custom source-available share-back license. Please read
the full license for the binding conditions. Thank you for respecting the project
and helping make it accessible to more players!
