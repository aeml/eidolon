# Suggested response to issue #20

Thank you for asking and for your interest in bringing Eidolon to Thai players!
I've clarified the licensing: the software uses [GNU AGPL v3](https://github.com/aeml/eidolon/blob/master/LICENSE),
and game assets use a separate [asset share-alike license](https://github.com/aeml/eidolon/blob/master/ASSET-LICENSE).
The [licensing guide](https://github.com/aeml/eidolon/blob/master/docs/LICENSING.md)
explains the details.

1. **Translation:** Yes, you may translate the UI, menus, items, quests, and other
   player-facing text into Thai or any other language and share your version.
2. **Code and features:** Yes, you may modify the source and add features.
   Follow AGPL's distribution requirements and, when hosting a modified version,
   prominently offer interacting players the complete corresponding source free
   of charge under AGPL. That includes covered changes and translation files.
   No direct submission or pull request to me is required; source access under
   the license allows upstream reuse. Contributions are welcome, but I am not
   obliged to merge them.
3. **Hosting:** Yes, you may host your own Thai-language server in Thailand.
4. **Attribution:** Preserve licenses, copyright, and third-party notices, and
   credit Robert Mendola as Eidolon's original developer and maintainer. For game
   assets, include "Based on Eidolon by Robert Mendola, original developer and
   maintainer" in accessible credits/about, linking here where supported. You may
   translate the credit and add your team's credits. Please identify the server
   as independently operated.
5. **Player models:** Yes, you may replace them. When distributing or publicly
   hosting an adapted Eidolon asset collection, share complete editable sources
   for integrated new and replacement models under the same asset license,
   including source scenes, rigs, textures, animations, and exports. That allows
   others, including the original project, to reuse them.
6. **Other assets:** The same terms apply to monsters, weapons, armor, equipment,
   mounts, and animations integrated into the adapted collection. Entirely
   independent artwork that neither uses nor adapts the licensed assets or their
   protected collection is outside the asset license's scope.

**Commercial use is allowed**, including cosmetics, mounts, convenience items,
monthly VIP subscriptions, virtual-item sales, and third-party payment
integrations. No separate project permission or revenue share is required for
compliant use. Share covered integrated payment code, but keep credentials,
private keys, player data, and transaction records private. Independent payment
providers' infrastructure need not be published.

Official Eidolon's philosophy is that money buys cosmetics, never gameplay
power. I will not merge features that sell gameplay advantages. Your independent
fork may choose differently, provided it meets the applicable software and asset
source-sharing requirements, even for changes upstream would decline to merge.

Provide prominent links to matching code and editable asset sources as required
by the licenses. Asset sources must remain accessible while you offer the version
and for at least three years afterward. Use replacement assets you have rights
to share and license for reuse; existing third-party licenses still apply. You
remain responsible for payment-provider agreements and applicable laws.

Please read both licenses for their binding conditions. Thank you for respecting
the project and helping make Eidolon accessible to more players!
