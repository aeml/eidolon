# Fighter (Tank)
Tier 1 (Starting Skill)

• Charge (Splash Damage + Gap-Closer)
Rush forward, damaging and briefly staggering enemies in a small radius.

Branch A — Shield & Mitigation (Main Tank Path)

Tier 2: Bulwark Stance
Reduce incoming damage while in combat.

Tier 3: Shield Slam
AoE cone threat + minor stun.

Tier 4: Iron Fortress
Flat damage reduction for X seconds after Charge.

Tier 5: Guardian Roar
Large-radius taunt + group damage reduction buff.

Branch B — Control & Crowd Management

Tier 2: Sweeping Strike
Small AoE cleave that increases threat.

Tier 3: Earthshaker
Ground smash that knocks down nearby enemies.

Tier 4: Unbreakable Grip
Short-range pull (single target) to help cluster mobs.

Tier 5: Juggernaut Charge
After Charge, creates a shockwave that slows enemies heavily.

Branch C — Damage Tank / Offense-Tank Hybrid

Tier 2: Berserker Edge
Gain a % damage buff when at >60% HP.

Tier 3: Shattering Charge
Charge applies armor reduction to all hit.

Tier 4: Executioner Spin
AoE spin that deals higher damage to marked or taunted enemies.

Tier 5: Last Stand Rampage
When dropping below 30% HP, gain a massive damage boost for X seconds.

# Rogue (DPS Mobility + Piercing Damage)
Tier 1 (Starting Skill)

• Piercing Throw
Throw a dagger that pierces through enemies in a line.

Branch A — Assassin Burst Path (Single-Target DPS)

Tier 2: Backstab
Bonus damage from behind.

Tier 3: Weak Point Mark
Marked enemies take more damage from Piercing Throw.

Tier 4: Shadow Lunge
Teleport behind target → apply bleed.

Tier 5: Death Spiral
Finisher that deals heavy damage per bleed stack.

Branch B — Throwing Specialist Path (Ranged Physical DPS)

Tier 2: Ricochet Blades
Piercing Throw now bounces once.

Tier 3: Serrated Edges
Piercing hits apply bleed to all targets hit.

Tier 4: Blade Storm
Throw a fan of 5 daggers in a cone.

Tier 5: Phantom Volley
Rapid-fire three Piercing Throws in a row.

Branch C — Utility / Debuff Path

Tier 2: Smoke Bomb
AoE slow + reduces enemy accuracy.

Tier 3: Poison Coating
Small DoT + healing reduction.

Tier 4: Tripwire
Drop a trap that roots the first enemy touched.

Tier 5: Cloak & Vanish
Instant invisibility + massive movement speed burst.
