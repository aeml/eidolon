                    Open alpha · Known limits & reporting
                    
Eidolon is still in open alpha. Character art, equipment fit, environments and balance are improving; this is not the finished visual quality or a closed-beta launch.

                    
Performance: Try Low graphics in Settings if play feels uneven. Phone-sized layouts are tested, but sustained dungeon and party play on real phones is still being evaluated. No universal device or frame-rate guarantee.

                    
Story pacing: Campaign completion time and group difficulty are playtest targets, not measured guarantees. Report stalled quests, unreachable locations or unclear encounter instructions.

                    

                        
Progression playtest goals · not guarantees

                        
First dungeon: Reach level 30 in roughly 2–3 hours on a fresh, unassisted character and enter Verdant Bastion with an appropriately geared party.

                        
Level 100: About 100 hours of ordinary progression, without depending on daily-quest grinding or high-level carries.

                        
Dark Realm: Another 8–12 hours of quests, lore and the dungeon leading to the Dark King raid. A well-geared five-player finale should take roughly 5–10 minutes.

                        
Forge: Moderate potency during the story; +20 is a long-term endgame goal, not an expected campaign requirement.

                        
Useful feedback: Include your class, start/end levels, approximate active time, solo/party play and any gifts or carries. Say where progress felt unclear, too slow or too generous. The optional timer below stays local unless you stop, review and explicitly share its summary.

                    

                    
Group play: Use Group Finder or your guild to arrange a tank, healer and damage dealers for dungeons and raids. Check the Dungeon Guide for entry requirements; every player claims their own ready quests. Returning to an active dungeon uses its latest cleared boss checkpoint; the 15-minute logout-resume window is unchanged.

                    
Population and network limits: Up to 100 future beta players is a planning target, not a promise of 100 simultaneous sessions. Current-build crowded multiplayer headroom is still unverified. IPv4 deployment checks do not establish that every IPv6 network can reach the game.

                    
Need help? Use Menu → Report Bug / Feature. Include what happened, what you expected and how to repeat it. Build and area are included automatically; optional diagnostics stay off unless you select them. Do not include passwords or payment information.

                    
Keep your report reference after the server confirms it was saved. Administrators can review it in-game; submitting does not promise an immediate reply or automatically punish another player.

                    
Future beta: Closed beta waits until the game is nearly complete. Existing accounts and characters retain access and progress; new beta players will be invited. Public alpha access is unchanged.

Starting Your Journey

                    
Touch combat: Move with the joystick. Tap an enemy to select it, then press Attack, Skill or a hotbar ability. Tap overlapping enemies again to cycle the selection. Tap empty ground or the target's × button to cancel pursuit. Without a selected enemy, directional skills cast ahead of your hero. Tap a lore object or press Use nearby to read it. Pinch the world to zoom; Menu → Reset Camera restores the default view.

                    
1. Meet Archmage Ilyra in town to accept your Fourfold Chronicle beneath her gold quest marker. Visit the Quest Giver outside the Forge for blue-marked daily contracts. Return to the appropriate giver and click Complete Quest to receive your rewards.

                    
2. Open Inventory (I) to compare and equip useful drops. Favor Strength for Fighters, Dexterity for Rogues, Intelligence for Wizards and Wisdom for Clerics; Vitality helps every class survive. Use Vendor / Repair for obvious junk, Stash for anything you may keep, and Forge for Shards, Hearts, and Gems.

                    
Train: Open Skill Tree (K), spend earned training points, and place learned abilities on your hotbar. On phones, use Menu → Skills & Runes and the bag button instead of keyboard shortcuts.

                    
Recover: Anywhere inside Lanternhold restores 10% of health and mana each second. Time in town builds Well Rested, up to two hours: 10% more stats and 25% more enemy-kill XP. Its timer runs only outside safe zones. Roadside camps are not safe zones.

                    
Explore: The atlas’s Places & lore filter shows public camps, landmarks and optional readings. Select a destination for a personal waypoint; this does not move your hero. Journal tracking controls which quest objectives appear. Optional lore does not replace Ilyra’s saved investigations or grant quest rewards.

                    
3. Level 30 unlocks Verdant Bastion Catacombs. Later realms have higher entry requirements; check the Dungeon Guide before setting out.

                    
4. Level 100 unlocks Heroic and Mythic dungeon difficulties.

                    
5. If you get turned around, reopen World Map (M) or Journal (J) to recover the route.

                

                

                    
Your Next Adventure

                    
At your pace: Continue Ilyra’s Chronicle through the Journal. Story progress does not reset. After completing the Dark King quest, return to Ilyra for A Letter Without a Throne, an optional conversation—not another daily obligation.

                    
Along the roads: Elemental disturbances rotate every 10 minutes through Earth, Water, Fire and Air. Check the World Map for the current site. Each window begins with one minute of warning and ends at minute eight; a victory calms nearby hazards for five minutes. Join when it suits you; there is no daily attendance reward.

                    
Optional daily hunts: Blue-marked contracts reset at midnight Eastern Time, including daylight-saving changes. The Journal’s reset countdown helps you turn in ready contracts before reset. Dailies supplement your adventures; they are not required to continue the story.

                    
A weekly raid: Once you qualify, organize a Dark Realm group through Group Finder or your guild. Its personal cache resets Monday at 00:00 UTC; the Dungeon Guide shows your eligibility. After claiming it, you can still help friends without receiving a second weekly cache.

                    
A season to improve: Arena and guild dungeon seasons follow calendar quarters, beginning January, April, July and October at 00:00 UTC on the first day. Check the arena for qualification, rewards and your season record. Earned Honor remains yours; seasonal rankings are not a character or story wipe.

                    
Together, or a quieter evening: Party members share kill credit throughout the same dungeon; in the overworld, stay within roughly two screens of the fight. Everyone turns in their own quests. Find companions, work on a build, trade at the Trading House, or visit the casino for optional entertainment—not guaranteed income. You do not need to finish every activity each day.
